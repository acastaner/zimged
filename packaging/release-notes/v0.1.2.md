Clearer feedback while editing: the pointer and the handles now show what a click or drag will do,
and you can pan a zoomed image with the wheel.

## Editor

- The mouse pointer changes to match what a press would do: a resize pointer over a selection or
  crop handle, a move pointer over a selected annotation. It updates live while you hold a
  modifier, so Shift over a handle shows the resize pointer before you press.
- The handle under the pointer highlights, so it is clear which one you will grab.
- When an image is zoomed in past the window fit, the wheel pans the view instead of stepping to
  the next image in the folder. At fit or smaller, the wheel steps through the folder as before.
