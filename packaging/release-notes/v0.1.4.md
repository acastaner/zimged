Sharper annotation editing: lines and arrows now have grab handles at each end, right-click drops
whatever tool you are using, and the window wears zimged's own icon.

## Annotations

- A selected line or arrow now shows a handle at each end instead of a bounding box. Drag either
  end to change its length or direction; hold Shift to snap the angle to 15 degrees, the same as
  when you draw it. Dragging the line itself still moves the whole thing.
- Right-clicking while a drawing tool is active returns you to Select, abandoning any shape you were
  part way through. In Select mode a right-click does nothing.

## Window

- The application window and title bar now show zimged's own icon rather than the toolkit's default
  placeholder, matching the icon your taskbar already uses.
